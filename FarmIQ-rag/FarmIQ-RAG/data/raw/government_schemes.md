# Government Agricultural Schemes (India) — Overview

FarmIQ Starter Reference — a general overview of well-known central government
schemes for farmers. Scheme rules, benefit amounts, and eligibility change over
time; always confirm current details on the official portal or at your nearest
Common Service Centre, bank, or agriculture office before applying.

## PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

- Provides direct income support to eligible landholding farmer families,
  paid in instalments directly to the bank account.
- Requires registration and Aadhaar-linked bank account details.
- Confirm the current instalment amount and eligibility on the official
  PM-KISAN portal.

## PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)

- Crop insurance against losses from natural calamities, pests, and diseases.
- Farmers pay a modest share of the premium; the government subsidises the
  rest.
- Enrolment is usually through banks, cooperatives, or the national crop
  insurance portal, within notified cut-off dates for each season.
- Report crop loss promptly to the insurer/bank to claim.

## Soil Health Card Scheme

- Provides farmers with soil-test-based information on nutrient status and
  crop-wise fertiliser recommendations.
- Aims to improve fertiliser use efficiency and reduce unnecessary spending.
- Soil samples are collected and tested, and a card is issued with
  recommendations.

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

- Provides farmers with timely access to short-term credit for crop inputs and
  other needs, at concessional interest with prompt-repayment incentives.
- Available through banks; may also cover animal husbandry and fisheries needs
  in many cases.

## Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)

- Focuses on expanding irrigation coverage ("Har Khet Ko Pani") and improving
  water-use efficiency ("Per Drop More Crop").
- Supports micro-irrigation (drip and sprinkler) with subsidies; apply through
  the state agriculture/horticulture department.

## e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)

- An online trading platform linking mandis to help farmers get better price
  discovery and access to more buyers.
- Farmers can sell produce through participating mandis and registered
  traders.

## Other useful supports

- **Agricultural extension and KVKs** — free or low-cost technical advice,
  demonstrations, and training.
- **Kisan Call Centre** — telephone advisory service for farming queries.
- **State-specific schemes** — many states run additional subsidy and support
  programmes; check your state agriculture department.

## How to use these schemes

1. Identify the schemes relevant to your crops and needs.
2. Check current eligibility and documents required (identity, land records,
   bank account).
3. Apply within the notified timelines — especially for insurance.
4. Keep copies of applications and receipts.
5. Ask your local agriculture office or KVK if anything is unclear; be cautious
   of anyone charging money to "guarantee" a scheme benefit.
